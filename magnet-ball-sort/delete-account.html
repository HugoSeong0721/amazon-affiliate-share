🧲 Delete your Magnet Balls account

App: Magnet Balls (io.github.hugoseong0721.magnetballs) · Developer contact: hugo.se@iottie.com

Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Magnet Balls.
  2. Tap ☰ (top right) to open the Journey screen.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

Your account and cloud progress are removed immediately.

Don’t have the app anymore?

Email hugo.se@iottie.com from the Google account address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Magnet Balls account”. We verify the sender address and delete the account within 7 days, then confirm by reply.

What gets deleted: your email address, display name, account identifier, and cloud-saved progress (levels and stars). This is all the data the game holds about you.

What stays: progress stored locally on your phone — it belongs to the device and is removed when you uninstall the app. No data is retained on our side after deletion.
