🧲 Magnet Balls — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026 · App: Magnet Balls (io.github.hugoseong0721.magnetballs)

The short version: you can play the entire game without an account, and in that case we collect no personal information — your progress stays on your device. Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, we receive your email address so we can save your progress in the cloud. You can delete your account (and everything we hold) at any time, right inside the game.

Playing without an account

No personal data is collected. Your level progress and stars are stored only on your device (local storage) and never leave it.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, we receive and store:

  • your Google account email address and display name,
  • a Google account identifier,
  • your game progress (levels cleared and stars).

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and is used to:

  • save your progress in the cloud so you can continue on a new phone, and
  • occasionally email you about updates to this game or new games we make. Every such email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing never affects your game.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the Google services named above that host it.

Advertising

The app may show ads served by Google AdMob. Ad networks may use your device’s advertising ID to limit repeat ads and measure performance. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses data in its advertising services.

Data retention & deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. To delete it:

  • In the game: open the Journey screen (☰) → Delete account → confirm. This immediately removes your account and cloud progress.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Progress saved locally on your phone stays on the phone until you uninstall the app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
