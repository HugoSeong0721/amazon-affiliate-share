🧩 Privacy Policy

Slang Crossword · Effective date: July 29, 2026

TL;DR — we collect nothing. Slang Crossword has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your game progress never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Slang Crossword does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your own device only, using your browser's local storage:

  • Which levels you have cleared or unlocked
  • Your language preference (English / Korean)

This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data (or uninstalling the app) deletes it permanently.

Payments

Optional in-game purchases (level unlocks) are processed entirely by a third-party payment provider. We never see or store your card number or billing details. The payment provider's own privacy policy applies to the checkout process.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Reach the developer via the project's GitHub page.
